Welcome to our brand-new home on the web! Here's what you'll find here:

- **Schedule** — every meeting and campout, always current (it comes straight from our troop Google Calendar)
- **Announcements** — sign-up deadlines, event news, and reminders like this one
- **Photos** — highlights from recent adventures
- **Documents** — packing lists, permission slips, and forms
- **Contact** — how to reach us and how to join

Thinking about joining? Boys ages 11–17 can join at any time of year —
