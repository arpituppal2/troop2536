Classic Scouting, youth-led.

Troop 2536 is a Scouts BSA troop chartered in Mountain House, California, serving girls ages 11 through 17. Our program is classic Scouting: monthly outings, rank advancement, community service, and a youth-led structure where scouts take real responsibility with trained adults backing them up.

Leadership

In Scouts BSA, the scouts run the troop. The Senior Patrol Leader and the Patrol Leaders' Council plan the year; adults coach, keep everyone safe, and handle charter requirements.

What we do

  • Monthly campouts — tent camping, backpacking, and council camps across Northern California.
  • Rank advancement — from Scout through Eagle, at each scout's own pace.
  • Service — Mountain House community projects throughout the year.
  • High adventure — the long-term prize: places like Philmont and Sea Base.

Join us

Any girl age 11+ (or finished 5th grade) can join at any meeting of the year — there's no "season." The best first step is visiting a meeting. Head to the Contact page and email us so we know you're coming.
